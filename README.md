# All Black Limo — Luxury Chauffeur & Black Car Homepage

A modern, fully responsive demo homepage designed for **All Black Limo**, a premier executive black car and limousine service based in Seattle, WA. Crafted with an obsidian-and-champagne luxury aesthetic, semantic HTML5, Tailwind CSS, and vanilla JavaScript.

## Live Demo
> https://beautiful-torrone-967997.netlify.app

## Key Features

- **Responsive Navigation & Mobile Drawer:** Sticky glassmorphism header with a custom geometric monogram emblem, quick dispatch phone link, and an accessible mobile menu drawer.
- **Interactive Quick Quote Engine:**
  - Instant pickup and drop-off inputs with one-click preset pills (*SeaTac Airport*, *Downtown Seattle*, *Pier 66*, *Pier 91*, *Bellevue*).
  - Auto-recommended or manual vehicle class selection based on passenger count.
  - Real-time flat-rate and hourly charter estimate modal with booking confirmation flow.
- **Tailored Chauffeur Services:** Dedicated cards for SeaTac Airport Transfers, Pier 66 & 91 Cruise Terminal Transfers, Corporate & Executive Travel, Weddings & Galas, and Hourly As-Directed Charters.
- **4-Tier Executive Fleet Showcase:** Interactive cards for *Executive Sedan*, *Luxury SUV*, *Mercedes Sprinter Van*, and *Luxury Minibus* that pre-fill the quote calculator upon selection.
- **Social Proof & Service Areas:** Verified client reviews and a structured footer detailing 24/7 dispatch contact details and Greater Seattle coverage areas.
- **Technical & Local SEO:** Includes semantic HTML5 landmarks, meta descriptions, Open Graph tags, descriptive image `alt` text, and JSON-LD `LimousineBusService` structured schema.

## Tech Stack

- **HTML5** (Semantic structure & JSON-LD Schema)
- **Tailwind CSS** (via CDN with custom luxury color palette & typography tokens)
- **Vanilla JavaScript** (Interactive quote calculator, modal logic, and mobile navigation)
- **Google Fonts** (*Cormorant Garamond* & *Plus Jakarta Sans*)
